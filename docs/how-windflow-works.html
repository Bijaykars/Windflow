How WindFlow works

Oct 29, 2026·@Bijay Karki

WindFlow blows wind through and around your building on the graphics card and reports, room by room, how fresh the air is. It is a design-comparison tool: it ranks options against each other on the same model. It does not certify a building.

From model to report in six steps

Figure 1 · The pipeline. Blue is fresh outdoor air, grey is air that has been indoors a while; the same colour code is used in every figure below.

Two numbers for every room

Fresh-air ACH Fresh-air ACH = 3600 ÷ mean age of air (s) How many times an hour the room’s air is replaced by air that is effectively fresh. Air arriving through other rooms counts at its real age.
Total exchange All air entering the room per hour, including air that came from other rooms. It is a measure of how much air moves, not a ventilation rate.
Read the two together. A room can have a lot of air moving through it and still little fresh air.

What the numbers are good for

Figure 3 · Trust the ranking, not the absolute number. Changing the cell size alone can move one room by up to about 35 % and the whole building by about 20 %, so never compare results from different grids.

Why a window’s size in cubes matters

A sharp-edged window lets air through with a discharge coefficient of about Cd ≈ 0.61. The grid has to reproduce that.

What to keep in mind

The ground is smoothWind near the ground is too weak: about 54 % under the Eurocode profile at 1 m.
The compact outdoor area crowds the buildingFlows read about 11–14 % high. Use Full for a final check.
Walls do not conduct heatNo U-values; walls, floors and glazing are adiabatic.
Small heat sources under‑stratifyWarm air from a small source does not layer as strongly as it would in reality.
Imported meshes read higherTotal exchange is 37–45 % higher than the same building drawn in the app. Compare imported with imported.
Not yet checked against a measured buildingBenchmarks are lab cases and published studies, not a real occupied building.

Full method, equations and every benchmark: WindFlow Technical Reference.

WindFlow is a design-stage tool. Its results are estimates with stated uncertainty, not a substitute for a ventilation engineer or a code-compliance calculation.
