# WindFlow solver validation

This page is the public record of what the WindFlow lattice-Boltzmann solver has been checked
against, and what it has not. It is generated by hand from `src/WindFlow.Sandbox`, the same
regression harness the solver must pass before any release. Each row gives the result of the run named in its
header; rows re-measured in the 2026-10-10 run carry that date, older rows say when they were measured. The
physics fixes of 2026-10-12 (wall positions, ground slab, gust scale, flux-controlled Return/Fan patches, and others) moved the numbers: rows marked
"not in queue V" were not re-measured after the physics fixes of 2026-10-12 and are shown with the last run's figure; the generated block below is rewritten by the next
validation run. No number on this page is a target. The measured-room rows say which outer ring they ran on, and the Sandbox wind rows do too (see "Outer ring per row"), and the FAC2 column is the Hanna & Chang W-rule count until a re-run under the literature definition (queue V ran it for the three measured cases; see "FAC2 and W").

**Queue V (2026-10-20).** The GPU re-run queue on the fixed tree (63 jobs; internal test logs; later reruns in Batches V2 and V3) re-measured the three measured-room cases, the heated cavity, the whole Sandbox suite (253 PASS, 3 FAIL) and the room-ACH runs. A row that queue V did not re-measure says "not in queue V". Jobs after 09:32 ran with the GPU clock capped at 2100 MHz, which gave 12.7 % fewer steps in a 600 s cap (225,700 against 258,532); it matters only for time-capped runs, and the validation jobs stop on their own convergence rule (all `converged True`).

## 1. What is validated, and what is not

**Checked (this page):** analytic and semi-analytic flow references (orifice equation,
Poiseuille channel, hydrostatic buoyancy; the sealed filling box, 19a, currently FAILS its 1.8-8.6 K band at 1.30 K: an under-resolved plume), a textbook CFD benchmark (the de Vahl Davis / Fusegi differentially heated cavity; its test geometry was fixed in Batch 4, P14-02, and queue V re-measured it on 2026-10-20: 31a-e pass),
internal self-consistency checks (mass conservation, CPU
vs. GPU parity, mean-age identities, scalar transport, nested-grid agreement), and device-level
mass/energy balance (fans, split AC, exhaust, diffusers).

**Compared against measurements, mixed result:** three measured cases have been run -- the IEA Annex 20 2D room,
the Tominaga-Blocken (2015) cross-ventilation wind tunnel and the MINIBAT ceiling jet (Kuznik 2008). In their last
runs on the fixed code (queue V, 2026-10-20) one meets all four metrics (cross-ventilation at H = 40 cells with measured approach turbulence; at H = 20 it fails) and two miss the hit-rate q
by a margin (MINIBAT by 0.017, Annex 20 by 0.207); see *Latest measured-room numbers* and *Known limitations* below.
No measured room has been used to check the thermal model (see Known limitations).
Planned, not done:

- **IEA Annex 20** 3-D mixing and displacement rooms with temperature (the isothermal 2-D case, which is the Nielsen (1990) test case, has been run)
- **Single-sided ventilation** cases (no reference exists here for a room with one opening)
- **AIJ** (Architectural Institute of Japan) guideline cases for wind-driven ventilation

Until then, treat WindFlow's absolute numbers (ACH, comfort fraction, room temperature) as
comparative and self-consistent, not as verified against a physical building.

### Grid convergence (P16-G4)

Queue V, 2026-10-20: Standard run, steady wind, sample house, product ring, 90-minute cap each (internal test logs). The three cell budgets 2.32 / 5.09 / 11.2 M gave 2.38 M cells at 220 mm, 5.19 M at 169 mm and 11.41 M at 130 mm (refinement ratio about 1.30). **Verdict: not grid-converged for room ACH; only Hall and Bedroom 1 fresh-air hold within 5 %.** No room converges monotonically on total exchange, and a grid-convergence index cannot be computed for a divergent or oscillatory series. **Dx jitter:** the same house and wind at 5.09 M cells / 170.4 mm (60-minute run, `V-P16-CONV`) and at 5.19 M cells / 169 mm (`V-GCI-5.09M`) differ by 0.6 % in Dx, yet fresh-air ACH moves Living 12.3 to 10.8 (-12 %), Kitchen 33.5 to 26.9 (-20 %) and Landing 46.0 to 57.1 (+24 %), and Bedroom 1 total exchange 123.3 to 168.0 (+36 %); both runs have tight ranges, so this is not noise. ## 4. Known biases (stated honestly)

- **Cross-ventilation flow rate reads high (tracer-tb16, V1, 2026-10-28: FAIL).** Tominaga & Blocken 2016 configurations A-E,
  40 cells per H, the cross-vent H40 box (4 H margins, 3.0 % blockage, 24.8 M cells), GPU: Q/(U_H A_in) by tracer A 0.588, B 0.529,
  C 0.496, D 0.447, E 0.522 against the paper's tracer values 0.37 / 0.27 / 0.23 / 0.22 / 0.25 (x1.59-2.16; band +-20 %: FAIL in all
  five). A is ranked first (PASS). Our tracer equals our velocity-integrated flux to 1 %. The set-up and normalisation match the paper
  (internal test log), but the reference itself reads low: for E (the TB2015 isolated building)
  TB2015 puts the tracer value 37 % below PIV opening flux (about 0.40), and an LES (Hwang & Gorle 2022) gives 0.44-0.47. Against those,
  E reads x1.11-1.33 high. A-D have no PIV/LES value. About +6 % of the gap is the 4 H box (the guideline box, 1.9 % blockage, would
  not fit at 40 cells: 17.1 GB). The band was not changed. Results: internal test log.
- **Opening flow is the carved hole's flow, and the carved hole is not the drawn one.** Since
  2026-09-28 an opening's flow rate is the normal velocity integrated over the throat cells the
  voxelizer actually carved (it used to be a box-mean speed times the drawn width x height, which read
  up to ~13% low on the stack scene and ~4% high on the cross scene). The carved hole is rounded to
  whole cells: at the checked grids it is 0-15% larger than drawn (1.152 vs 1.000 m² on the stack
  scene, 1.631 vs 1.44 m² on the two-storey scene, exact on the cross scene), so a coarse grid can
  pass more air than an opening of the drawn size would. Each opening now reports its carved area and
  an effective discharge coefficient on the drawn area; a value outside 0.42-0.8 (0.5-0.8 with the Resolved opening model) on an opening at least
  4 cells across raises a warning; narrower openings get a different note ("is only N cells across"). Refining the grid shrinks the rounding. Since batch 5 the throat the flow is divided by counts the same cells the carve opens (a hole whose edge lies on a cell centre opens, and counts, one cell more across that axis; RP follow-up 4).
- **The scalar (CO₂/age) solved region includes a 1 m outdoor ring** around the building, not just
  indoor air. Any check that integrates over "the whole solved region" (e.g. the age identity)
  is integrating over that ring too, not indoor air alone.
- **The thermal model is the hybrid finite-volume scheme** (D3Q19 flow plus a mass-consistent FV energy equation with
  a TVD advection limiter; the D3Q7 thermal lattice is kept only as an option). It is checked against textbook cases
  (3-D cavity against Tric et al. 2000 at n = 160, test geometry fixed in Batch 4 (P14-02), queue V 2026-10-20: 0.1 % / 0.5 %; the sealed filling box, which fails its 1.8-8.6 K band at 1.30 K), not against a
  measured room. The thermal walls are plain (halfway) bounce-back, not the interpolated Bouzidi model of the momentum
  checks.
- **Cavity Nusselt is under-resolved at coarse grids.** At n=48 the two Rayleigh cases read 8–10% below the 3-D
  reference (Hybrid; the D3Q7 scheme read 12–16 %); at n=160 they read 3-4 % low, but the cavity's hot and cold boxes also hold strips of the adiabatic walls at their temperature (P14-02), so the gap is not attributable to grid spacing alone.
- **Split AC / cassette return re-ingestion on coarse grids.** On a coarse grid, part of a device's
  own supply air can be counted again at its return grille before it has mixed with the room,
  biasing measured duty. Check 41f bounds the return against the air at its own height. Check 41g (an
  energy balance on a known load) replaced "return ≤ room mean + 0.5 K", which assumed mixing
  ventilation; a cold jet over warm air is stably stratified and a high return then reads above the mean.
  Its V̇ is the unit's set flow (what the heat sink acts on); the flow measured as the flux through the outlet face
  is 0.145 m³/s against the 0.15 set, and against that the expected return would be 17.44 °C (error +0.18 K).

- **Supply patches deliver their set flow at rho0, so volumetric flow reads 0.5–1% low.** The link inlet
  injects exactly FlowRate of mass (rho_w = rho0 = 1); the room sits a few tenths of a percent above rho0,
  so the mean velocity through a cross-section is that much below FlowRate/area (55a–55f read 0.991–0.999).
  The wall velocity is first scaled to the cells actually carved (drawn Width x Height over carved area,
  0.56 on the sandbox supply scene), then the controller corrects missing links within 0.8–1.6. Since batch 3b
  (2026-10-12) Return and Fan patches are link-wise and flux-controlled too; the far-field wind ring, and the Return/Fan patches of a sealed room whose set
  supply and extract differ by more than 1 %, still use the equilibrium reset at ρ0 = 1 (the sealed-room case keeps the room's pressure anchored; a room note says so).
- **Openings discharge at a calibrated Cd, not a resolved one (default `OpeningModel.Calibrated`).** The lattice
  resolves a sharp opening at Cd 0.48–0.69 depending on its width n and wall thickness t (n = 4: 0.48–0.60, n = 8: 0.62–0.65, n = 16: 0.65–0.69); where that is above 0.61 a sub-grid Forchheimer loss
  in the throat cells brings it to `Opening.DischargeCoefficient` (0.61 default). The `CdSolver(n, t)` table behind it is read on the product wall route (mesh walls), as the mean over eight placements of the hole
  against the grid (Batch 6d+6i, RP): the calibrated mean is within ±1.3 % of 0.61 for n = 8 and 16 and walls 0.5–4 cells thick. n = 4 resolves below 0.61, gets no loss and a note (flow about 1–21 % low); the per-placement scatter is 1.6–30 %
  and the app notes it above 5 % (README, Known limitations, item 4). Aligned (edge-on-face) and edge-on-centre holes lie outside that scatter X (58a: +17 % at n 8, t 1). Openings much wider than 16 cells, rounded or thick-reveal openings are not calibrated separately. The cross-ventilation figure Q/(UH·A) 0.552 → 0.463 is from before the fixes; queue V (2026-10-20) read 0.498 at H = 40 (inside the 0.36-0.52 band) and 0.544 at H = 20 (outside it). The BENCH orifice rig (6a, internal test log), which holds the hole at one offset, read Calibrated cd_rig 0.739 / 0.716 / 0.670 and Resolved 0.739 / 0.743 / 0.719 at 4 / 8 / 16 cells (lossK 0.000 / 0.145 / 0.326), none within 3 % of 0.61; it reads one placement of the scatter above, not the phase mean the calibration targets (internal test log, Batch 6d, follow-up 2), and at n = 4 the calibrated Cd was 0.611 before the fixes. The BENCH rotated room (6b, 6 cells, 0 / 30 / 45 degrees) read Calibrated 0.587 / 0.497 / 0.611 with ledger residual 0.024 / 0.012 / 0.010 (before: 0.008 / 0.269 / 0.508 at dx 0.30).

## 5. Known limitations

Stated before the pre-release audit, so the audit checks claims that match the code.

- **Annex 20 ceiling-jet decay.** The ceiling jet (profile y = h/2) decays about 25 % too fast at every grid tried, so Annex 20
  fails the hit rate (q 0.471 at s8, band 0.66). This is the open gap in that case.
- **No measured-room thermal validation.** Thermal (beta) is checked against textbook cases only: the 3-D differentially
  heated cavity (3-4 % below Tric et al. 2000 at n = 160 on 2026-10-10 with the old test geometry; 0.1 % / 0.5 % in the queue V run of 2026-10-20 after the fix), the sealed filling box (1.30 K against its 1.8-8.6 K band: a FAIL) and
  energy balances. No measured, heated, occupied room has been simulated. The filling box is under-resolved at the grid sizes the app uses (1.35 / 1.35 / 1.55 / 1.76 K at Dx 0.268 / 0.198 / 0.134 / 0.100 m, not converged), so absolute stratification is the least certain thermal output (about 25-40 % low).
- **Single-sided ventilation is not validated.** Every measured and benchmark case here has flow through two or more
  openings; a room with one opening has not been compared with any reference.
- **High mode gives wide ranges in gusty wind.** High adds EN 1991-1-4 gusts and supply turbulence, so each room's ACH varies
  batch to batch and the ranges are wide (the 10-minute sample-house run of 2026-10-09, before the fixes, left every room outside its 5 % aim, for example
  Hall 51.4 ± 10.1 ACH, Bedroom 1 119 ± 29 ACH; in queue V, 2026-10-20, a 30-minute High run on 5.19 M cells ended at its budget with none of the 6 open rooms settled, internal test log). A run too short to estimate a range (under 16 batches in all, or under 5 kept)
  shows no range at all rather than an absurd one.
- **Steady and gusty wind give different ACH.** On the sample house the Living room read 13.3 ACH with steady wind
  (Standard) against 20-23.5 ACH with gusty wind (High) (2026-10-09 figures, before the fresh-air ACH definition and the later fixes; not in queue V). Queue V compared the building fresh-air rate instead: 27.0 ACH online in High against 20.1 in Standard (+34 %), and 13.3 when High's mean flow is evaluated steadily (internal test logs). Steady wind under-reads rooms that are ventilated mainly by gusts (one opening, or openings only on walls parallel to the wind);
  for those rooms the gusty number is the more realistic one, though neither is validated. The app flags such rooms under Standard. Compare like with like and do
  not mix a Standard number with a High one.
- **The split AC delivers about 3 % below its set flow** (0.145 against 0.15 m³/s in check 41g, measured as the flux
  through the outlet face). An earlier reading above the set flow was the measurement, not the air: it projected the velocity on the
  tilted jet direction, which reads 1/cos(louvre angle) too high (P13a-01). Check 41g uses the set flow and would read +0.18 K against the measured one.
- **Openings under 4 cells across are unreliable.** The opening-flow calibration (Cd) is fitted for openings 4 cells or more
  across; narrower ones are not covered, and the Cd warning is skipped for them (a 4-cell opening already resolves below 0.61, 0.48-0.60, and a note gives the flow shortfall: §4, Batch RP). The app notes each one ("is only N cells across"). Refine the grid or use the
  simulation box. In Preview an opening 4-6 cells across has its size quantised to the grid (the carve is cell-centred), a ±1 cell row error of 15-25 % of its area; the app says
  so and points to Standard or High for that opening's flow.
- **Wall model is experimental and wind-only.** Off by default; ignored on thermal runs. On the measured cases it gave mixed, grid-dependent results (Annex 20 passes at s4, fails at s8).
- **Viscosity floor (P11-01).** The lattice viscosity is held at a stability floor, so on the app's building-scale grids the simulated air is about 150-850 times more viscous than real air
  (Standard ≈ 310×, Preview ≈ 470×; the validation cases run at 4-28×). Jets through windows and supplies are laminar-like (effective Re of a few hundred instead of ~10^5). Moving the floor to 1e-4
  changed the sample house's Living ACH by −30 % (13.4 → 9.4) and Kitchen by −15 %, and reversed the Kitchen window's flow. Disclosed, not fixed: a floor tied to measured stability is an open decision. The shipped floor stays 5e-4: in the pre-fix sweep at 1e-4 Annex 20 FAC2 read 0.604 against 0.575 (better), cross-vent NMSE 2.18 against 1.63 and MINIBAT FAC2 0.536 against 0.592 (worse). The rule was re-applied on the fixed code in queue V (2026-10-20, internal test log, 5e-4 to 1e-4): "WORSE: keep 5e-4." Annex 20 improves on all four metrics (FAC2 0.538 to 0.660, q 0.453 to 0.557, NMSE 0.356 to 0.205, FB 0.308 to 0.237), but cross-vent H = 40 gets worse on q (0.746 to 0.587) and NMSE (0.216 to 0.225), and MINIBAT on all four (FAC2 0.942 to 0.931, q 0.643 to 0.618, NMSE 0.083 to 0.089, FB 0.051 to 0.053).
- **The fine box sharpens the local view only (P11-02, Batch 6l).** Room ACH and opening flows always come from the whole-building run; a two-way coupled box was built and reverted. Against the uniform run at the box's Dx: w-hall-e opening 14.7 % (box) against 11.1 % (whole-building), Hall total ACH 12.7 % against 3.3 %; the two-way version read 16.0 % / 11.7 %. Check 48b stays in the table; a box-derived room or opening number is never shown as a result.
- **Walls are cut links on a cell-centre mask (P12-01, Batch 6a).** A wall crossing a link between two cell centres is a Bouzidi link at its exact q, from both sides, so a sub-cell wall is leak-tight (0 of 60 leak probes) and its position error is at most 0.065 cell (N = 8, offset 0.5: 0.104).
  The mesh-built Poiseuille order is 1.86–2.27 from N = 8 to 16 (diffusive 1.83–1.88) and 1.16–1.72 from 16 to 32 (the Mach-number floor). The cost is centre sampling: the LBM air volume of a room reads −6.3 to +2.1 % of nominal at Preview (254.5 mm) and −5.1 to +3.7 % at Accurate (169.6 mm), so room ACH divides by the nominal volume.
- **Indoor temperature is held by an ideal controller (P14-01, Batches 6j and S).** With Thermal on, each room's volume mean is held at the indoor value by an ideal heater/cooler (on by default; free-running is a switch). The occupied zone of a stratified room can differ (a note appears above 1 K), and a room with its own split AC is not held.
  Walls are adiabatic; only Surface sources exchange heat, by a correlation (Batch 6e). With the hold off, the typed indoor temperature is the initial air temperature only, and the stack effect decays during the pass (the time-mean indoor excess keeps 2-36 % of the typed ΔT in a well-mixed estimate).
- **± is statistical only (P16-01, P16-11).** It is the averaging range of the model's own time mean (95 %; the 99 % width the stop rule tested for a Settled room); grid, inflow and model error are not in it, and no grid-convergence
  study exists for room ACH. "Settled" cannot exclude a drift of up to about 1.5 times the aim; a room whose batches still drift is marked.
- **Sealed rooms with unbalanced devices.** A sealed room whose set supply and extract differ has no pressure anchor under flux-controlled Return patches, so the app scales the extract to the supply (both stay flux-controlled; with only one side both go to 0) and adds a room note naming the room (RW-01);
  a Diffuser in a sealed room brings in no outdoor air (RW-05). Real rooms need a leakage path or transfer opening.
- **Sized limits (Batch 5; each with its source in the README's Known limitations).**
  Domain: the default Compact domain has 10.5 % blockage on the sample house against about 2.4 % in COST 732's; at the Preview cell size (1.54 M cells) queue V (2026-10-20, internal test logs; Full: 81 kept batches, 3 of 6 rooms still unsettled) read its room total exchange +8 to +33 % (median about +16 %; Bedroom 2 the largest) and its building fresh-air rate +25 % (12.1 against 9.7 ACH) above Full's. The older Batch 6b+6c measurement read 7-16 % and 27 % (10.8 against 8.5 ACH) but its Full run had only 2 batches. Use Domain: Full for a final check (11.7 times the cells).
  Ground: smooth and no-slip, wind at 1 m about 54 % under the EN profile; oblique winds drift the profile 10.8-15.9 % at 2 m (old ring 12.1-18.2 %; Batch RZ); the gust intensity Iu reads 0.94-0.97 of the EN target over 120 L/U (Batch RZ).
  Heat: walls are adiabatic and only Surface sources exchange heat (correlation checked against hand-computed plate values 3.413 / 4.448 W/m2K, check RT-10b; the cavity checks 31a-e run the older face rule and do not test it); stratification from small sources reads about 25-40 % low (19a, above).
  Age: outdoor air is age 0, so re-entrained exhaust counts as fresh (house within 9 % of a tracer, Batch 6f+6k).
  Boundary: an open-top user box keeps the older equilibrium ring (a velocity inlet through a cut wall diverged); without gusts the density noise is 9.6e-5 against 6.3e-5 (1.5 times; with gusts +1.6 %; Batch RZ, 270 degrees).
  Supply turbulence: the transfer table covers patches 4-16 cells across (2.06 / 1.41 / 1.31 / 1.17 / 1.22 at n 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16, clamped outside); below 4 cells the delivered turbulence is below the request (3 cells about 0.35-0.5, 2 cells axial only) and the app says so; total momentum reads +3 to +9 % at Tu 0.19 (Batch RP).
  Not yet addressed: the Cd of rotated (non-axis-aligned) openings (P12-06).

## 6. Disclaimer

WindFlow is an early-design guidance tool. These checks establish that the solver is
self-consistent, mass- and energy-conserving, and within a stated tolerance of a handful of
analytic and published-benchmark references. They are not a substitute for measured-building
validation (Annex 20 / Nielsen / Karava-Tominaga / AIJ: run, but no case is claimed as a pass now: the one that met its metrics awaits a re-run on the fixed code — see Section 1), and they
are not an engineering sign-off. Do not use WindFlow results as the sole basis for a code
compliance, life-safety, or contractual ventilation decision.
