H.264 VIDEO ENCODING: WindFlow does not ship a video codec. MP4 export calls the Media Foundation sink
writer and the H.264 encoder that are part of Windows, through the thin Vortice.MediaFoundation wrapper.
No encoder binary is redistributed with WindFlow, and playback/decoding likewise uses the operating
system. Any H.264 patent licensing therefore rests with the Windows installation, not with this
application. Video export is unavailable on a Windows install without the Media Feature Pack (N/KN
editions), where the sink writer returns an error that the app surfaces in the status strip.
